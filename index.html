Our Mission

Replace the inspection vessel.
Permanently.

Offshore wind farms are entering a critical phase — turbines approaching 25-year design lives, with foundations corroding silently under the waterline. The current solution is expensive, infrequent, and carbon-heavy. We built a better one.

Sunken Robotics deploys AI-powered autonomous drones that live inside wind farms — docked on the turbine structure itself, charged from the turbine grid, and continuously collecting the structural data operators need to make confident, defensible decisions.

Technology

Five steps.
No vessel required.

01

Deploy from the turbine

The drone launches from a docking station fixed to the turbine's J-tube or transition piece. It charges directly from the turbine's own power grid — no support vessel, no crew, no weather dependency.

02

Navigate autonomously

Onboard SLAM navigation guides the drone between monopiles, covering up to 5 turbines per operational cycle with no remote pilot.

03

Capture everything

4K video, ultrasonic wall-thickness readings, and cathodic protection potential measurements — the full structural picture, every time.

04

Upload automatically

On return to dock, the full data package auto-uploads to the onshore AI pipeline via the turbine's communications link. Zero manual handling.

05

Act on AI-prioritised reports

The AI engine flags cracks, corrosion risk, biofouling, and scour — ranked by urgency. Engineers get actionable recommendations, not raw video files.

Sustainability

Offshore wind is green.
Its maintenance shouldn't undermine that.

Every traditional inspection campaign dispatches a diesel-powered offshore support vessel. A single vessel campaign releases an estimated 30–60 tonnes of CO₂ — for one wind farm, one visit. With thousands of turbines requiring recurring inspections, the cumulative footprint is significant.

Sunken Robotics is powered by the wind farm itself. Zero vessel fuel. Zero mobilisation emissions. By enabling continuous monitoring, we also prevent premature decommissioning — the highest-carbon outcome of all.

Turbine-powered operation

Drone charges from the wind turbine grid — zero fossil fuel consumption during inspection cycles.

Vessel campaigns eliminated

Estimated 30–60 tCO₂ saved per campaign per wind farm by replacing offshore support vessels.

Lifetime extension enabled

Continuous condition data supports confident life-extension decisions — preventing early decommissioning, the most carbon-intensive outcome.
